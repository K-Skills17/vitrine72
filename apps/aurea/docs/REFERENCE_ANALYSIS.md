## Working principles (from the brief, applied independently)

Until the references are reviewed, ÁUREA's direction rests on the brief's stated art direction and general practice in premium dermatology/aesthetic branding:

1. **Architecture as brand.** Premium clinics sell the room as much as the procedure. The clinic environment gets real editorial space, not a gallery strip.
2. **The professional is the product.** Trust in Brazil's aesthetic market is built on the named, registered professional. Profiles, responsibility and evaluation process carry more weight than claims.
3. **Restraint signals price.** Fewer words, fewer colours, more whitespace. Hierarchy comes from scale and placement, not decoration.
4. **Evaluation, not purchase.** Conversion language centres on *avaliação*, never on buying a procedure. This also matches advertising rules.
5. **Treatment pages are landing pages.** Paid and organic traffic lands there; each must carry clinic identity, professional responsibility and a contextual CTA on its own.

## How ÁUREA differentiates

- **Name-led system:** "Áurea" (golden ratio / proporção áurea) gives a structural idea: layouts built on a 1 : 1.618 column split and a modular type scale on the same ratio. It is a composition rule, not a gold-coloured decoration.
- **Ivory and espresso, not pink and gold.** Avoids the most common Brazilian clinic palette (rosé + dourado).
- **Editorial index for treatments:** a numbered, typographic index instead of image cards, with imagery used sparingly where it adds information.
- **Process before persuasion:** the patient journey (contact → evaluation → plan → follow-up) is a primary section, not a footnote.
